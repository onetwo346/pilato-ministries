OUR STORY

Built on prayer. Carried by power.

Pilato Ministries is a house of prayer, prophecy and worship in Accra, Ghana — raised to carry the presence of God to every home it reaches.

01

We exist to bring people into a real, tangible encounter with God — through prayer, prophetic ministry and worship that carries weight.

What began as a small gathering of believers committed to prayer has grown into a house that many now call home. Every service, every altar call, and every prophetic word is shaped by one conviction: that God is still speaking, and still moving.

Pilato Ministries is rooted in Accra, but the house travels far beyond it — through livestreams, radio, and a community that gathers online as much as it does in person. Wherever you are, you're welcome at the altar.
